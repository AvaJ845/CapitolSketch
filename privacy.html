CapitolSketch

Privacy Policy

CapitolSketch collects nothing.

Effective September 9, 2026

There is no account, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party code in this app. Nothing you do in it is sent anywhere.

What we collect

Nothing. CapitolSketch has no sign-in and no user accounts. It contains no analytics, no crash-reporting SDK, no advertising SDK, and no third-party frameworks of any kind. Its Apple App Privacy label is Data Not Collected.

What stays on your device

Everything you set in the app is stored only on your device, in the app's private storage (an iOS App Group container). It is never uploaded, and we never see it:

  • the ticker symbols on your watchlist
  • the members you follow
  • which disclosures you have already seen (so alerts don't repeat)
  • your appearance and feed-order preferences

Deleting the app deletes all of it. There is no server copy because there is no server.

Network activity

To show new filings, the app contacts one host: the U.S. House Clerk's public disclosure server (disclosures-clerk.house.gov). It downloads the Clerk's public filing index and individual public PDF filings — the same files for every user. No request the app makes is shaped by your watchlist, your followed members, or anything else about you. Tapping a "view the source filing" link opens that public document in Safari.

Senate disclosure data is compiled into the app before release and ships inside it; the app does not contact the Senate system at runtime.

Notifications

If you turn on watchlist alerts, the app schedules local notifications on your device when a newly downloaded public filing matches a ticker or member you chose. The matching happens on your device, against data already on it. No notification service receives your list.

Children

The app is rated 4+ and is safe for all ages. Because it collects no data, it collects no data from children.

Data sharing and sale

We do not share, sell, rent, or trade personal information, because we do not have any. There is no data to disclose to third parties, and none has ever been transferred.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this URL with a new effective date. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
